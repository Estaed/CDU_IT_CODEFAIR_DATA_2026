# 20-community spike — result

Run 2026-09-12 (14:20–15:40). Code, inputs and outputs in `spike/`; the joined table is
`spike/out/capability_table.csv` (96 rows × 105 columns). Every number below was computed by
`spike/merge.py` from lane outputs, not typed in.

## Verdict: the combination survives; the spine has to move

Kill criterion from the arena verdict: *"the capability table shows no variation across the 96
communities (all satellite, all covered, all fail the same service), so the map is one colour"*.
Tested on all 96 BushTel Major/Minor communities, with 20 reported in detail.

| Clause | Result | Holds? |
|---|---|---|
| all satellite | 95 of 96 are outside both NBN fixed-line and fixed-wireless footprints; Yirrkala alone is fixed line (Nhulunbuy polygon) | **yes — the fixed column is one colour** |
| all covered | four publishers agree "covered" for 54, agree "not covered" for 11, **disagree for 31 (32 %)** | no |
| all fail the same service | telehealth video by the fixed-latency rule: 1 green, 58 amber, 11 red, 26 no clinic | partly — the colour is carried by the mobile path, not the fixed one |

So the D-half as written in the verdict ("NBN technology by footprint → latency → service
verdict") is nearly uniform and would colour the map one shade. The A-half (source agreement
count) has three times the disagreement the kill threshold asked for. **What varies across the
NT is the mobile path and what the publishers say about it; the NBN satellite floor is the same
everywhere.** The PRD should make the capability column "best available path per community"
(terrestrial mobile where a publisher and a licensed site agree, satellite otherwise), with the
service verdict computed on that path. Distinct (fixed, agreement count, telehealth, voice)
combinations over 96: 16.

## Findings that change the PRD

1. **The four mobile publishers disagree on 31 of 96 communities.** Patterns (ACCC 2025 4G
   polygon / NTG 2022 list / ACMA licensed site within 5 km / BushTel "Mobile phone" = Y):

   | Pattern | n | Reading |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1/1/1/1 | 54 | unanimous covered |
   | 0/0/0/0 | 11 | unanimous not covered (Areyonga 277 people, Haasts Bluff, Gan Gan, Dhalinybuy, Gurrumuru…) |
   | 1/0/1/1 | 10 | carrier map, licence and BushTel say yes; **NTG 2022 list is stale** |
   | 0/0/1/0 | 8 | **a licensed carrier site sits in the community (9–20 m monopoles at 40–850 m) but no publisher shows coverage** |
   | 1/1/0/1 | 3 | listed as "proximity to cell" by NTG; nearest site 5–18 km (Amoonguna, Nturiya, Daguragu) |
   | 0/0/1/1 | 3 | licensed site + BushTel yes, no carrier polygon, not listed |
   | other | 4 | |

   The 11 "licensed site but no ACCC polygon" communities (Baniyala, Mapuru, Manmoyi, Rorruwuy,
   Birany Birany, Woodycupaldiya, Orrtipa-Thurra, Amanbidji, Nitjpurru, Rittarangu, Wurankuwu)
   are the "verify on the ground" list the verdict predicted — either small cells licensed but
   not on air, not yet in the 2025 carrier prediction, or not mobile at all (Wurankuwu's is a
   broadcast site). The 14 "ACCC yes, NTG 2022 no" communities (Finke, Laramba, Nyirripi,
   Willowra, Wilora, Yuelamu, Canteen Creek, Wutunugurra, Milyakburra, Tara, …) are the refresh
   DCDD's own list needs; 9 of them were "N" in NTG's 2021 gazetteer.
2. **ACMA's 40 km radius carries no information (96/96), 5 km does (78/96).** The NTG 2022 list
   itself rests on "proximity to cell" for 12 of its 60 matches, so a 40 km claim is the
   government's inference, not an observation. Publisher 3 is defined at 5 km.
3. **BushTel's `Capabilities` flags are all true for all 96** and its "Mobile phone" row links
   to Telstra's coverage map — it is Telstra's claim restated, not an independent source. Keep
   it as a publisher (it is what a resident sees on the government portal) but say so.
4. **NBN footprint ≠ what the clinic uses.** The NBN residual describes consumer premises. NT
   health centres and schools sit on government enterprise links; the 2019 NTG list says 30 of
   these 96 communities have optic-fibre backhaul and 16 microwave (50 unlisted). A fibre-fed
   community's clinic is unlikely to be on Sky Muster. The service verdict must be computed on
   the best available path, and the report must say which path the clinic is assumed to use.
   Open question for DCDD: does NTG publish the health/education WAN technology per community?
5. **Three communities sit just outside a town footprint**: Gunyangara 6.8 km from the Nhulunbuy
   fixed-line polygon, Amoonguna 4.7 km from Alice Springs, Binjari 9.1 km from Katherine. All
   three get three 4G carriers and NBN satellite. That is a concrete, defensible recommendation
   ("extend the town footprint N km") for the Recommendations section.
6. **Fragility inputs exist and vary**: wet-season road cut noted by BushTel for 26 of 96;
   backhaul fibre 30 / microwave 16 / unknown 50. Cyclone exposure was not tested (BoM licence
   TBD).
7. **Population behind the verdicts**: telehealth red 1,071 people across 11 communities;
   amber 33,409 across 58. Small numbers on the red list — the story is "the amber majority
   depends on one Telstra site", not "thousands have nothing".

## Data facts worth carrying into Blueprint

- ACCC KML: Optus 4G 2025 is 1,142,260 placemarks (1.42 GB unzipped from 206 MB) and takes
  ~40 min to stream with lxml; Telstra 4G is 15,808 placemarks in 8 min; TPG/MOCN/5G under a
  minute each. Telstra 3G Outdoor 2025 does not exist (2024 is the last). Optus 5G (760 MB) and
  TPG 5G were not fetched. The pipeline must cache the NT clip once; never re-parse nationally.
- NBN fixed-wireless polygons in the NT are Darwin rural only (348 polygons, lon 130.6–131.4);
  fixed line covers Darwin, Palmerston, Katherine, Tennant Creek, Alice Springs, Jabiru,
  Nhulunbuy (35 polygons). Both CC BY 4.0, dated 2024-03-26.
- ACMA RRL join reproduced the research counts exactly: 464 NT cellular-band sites, Telstra 333
  / Optus 162 / TPG-Vodafone 81 / Mobile JV 0. Frequency is in Hz; keep STATUS = 1 (Granted).
  Attribution line required: "Based on Australian Communications and Media Authority information."
- NTG lists matched by normalised name + BushTel aliases: 2022 → 60 of 96, 2021 with-mobile → 57,
  2019 → 46, small cell → 2. 88 COMMUNITY-type rows in the 2022 list are not among the 96
  Major/Minor (homelands, Borroloola-type towns); the app's universe should stay BushTel's 96
  plus whatever the 2022 list adds, decided in the PRD.
- Three premises in the spike brief were wrong and the data corrected them: Kalkarindji is in
  the 2022 list; Baniyala and Mapuru have their own Telstra-licensed masts; Kintore is inside
  Telstra's 4G polygon. Nothing in the spec was forced to match.
