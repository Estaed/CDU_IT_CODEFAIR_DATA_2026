AssumptionNote: the printed assumption beneath a Degraded verdict, a grey block with a 3px ochre left rule; the only non-verdict use of a verdict colour.

```jsx
<AssumptionNote text="Could work over Telstra 4G if latency is under `100 ms`. No measurement exists here." />
```

Use only for verdicts that rest on a stated assumption. Never for general notes or warnings.
