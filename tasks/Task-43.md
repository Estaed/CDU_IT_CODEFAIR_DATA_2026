## Why

PRD §4.1 item 3 and §4.3: the report's Findings and Recommendations are built by Emma, Thanh
and Will from what the pipeline emits, and the report is the one deliverable the judges read
before they see the app (`docs/report-requirements.md`: 8 pages, Findings with "supporting
evidence", Recommendations "actionable, for government, industry or community"). Since

model, the priority list, the sensitivity table) with no figure or table addressed to the
report, and no document says which artefact proves which sentence. This task closes that
gap so the writing can start from evidence rather than from memory.

## Contract

1. **Figures** (PNG, same size, outline, tokens and source line as the three existing maps;
   every colour through the tokens; no literal):
   - `map_reliability.png`: the 96 points by `claim_reliability.word` (four fill states, the
     same glyph language as the app: `high` filled, `medium` half, `low` ring, `none` dotted
     ring), legend with counts.
   - `map_publishers.png`: the 96 points by the number of publisher lines that make a claim
     and say `covered` (0..5), with the three measured non-alignment communities marked.
   - `chart_sensitivity.png`: one horizontal bar chart, one bar per weight and factor, bar
     length the top-10 overlap (0..10), rho printed at the bar end; the two zero-weight
     components shown and labelled `awaiting OQ3` / `awaiting OQ5`.
2. **Tables** (CSV under `data/out/tables/`, header row, no free text beyond pack strings):
   - `top10.csv`: rank, name, region, population, score, intervention, addressee, why,
     telehealth verdict, reliability word, agreement `covered/available`.
   - `intervention_counts.csv`: intervention, addressee, count, population sum, communities
     with a health centre.
   - `publisher_lines_summary.csv`: per publisher (five rows): says covered, says not covered,
     not recorded, kind, source, date.
   - `reliability_words.csv`: word, count, population sum, mean `p_wrong`, the three most
     frequent driver pairs.
   - `verdict_counts.csv` (exists): add a population column per verdict per service if it
     is not there already.
3. **`docs/FINDINGS.md`**: one section per report section that needs evidence (Methodology,
   Findings, Discussion, Recommendations). Each entry is one sentence the report may make,
   the artefact that supports it (file path), and the number as the pipeline emitted it on
   this run, with the date. Every number in the file is copied from a table by the script
   that writes it, not typed: `figures.write_findings_md` reads the tables and renders the
   file, so re-running the pipeline refreshes it. Include the honesty sentences the report
   must not skip: the AUC rests on three folds; `low` is a rank, not a probability; 3 of 96
   have a drive test within 5 km; 95 of 96 verdicts rest on the Sky Muster figure and the
   LEO sentence; the licence status of every source (from `PROVENANCE.md`).
4. **README**: the "Run the pipeline" paragraph lists the figures and tables by name and says
   `docs/FINDINGS.md` is regenerated by the run.
5. Nothing new is computed: every figure and table is a projection of `capability_table.csv`,
   `priority.csv`, `reliability.csv`, the pack and the existing tables.
